Terms of Service & Operational Standards

Zero Spot delivers professional cleaning and air-conditioning engineering under a transparent, consumer-first charter. Review our clear zero-advance and digital invoicing terms below.

Direct Invoicing via Email

Users pay exclusively through official email invoice. You will never be asked for cash or upfront payment during booking or arrival. Upon 100% completion and inspection of your clean, an itemized GST invoice and secure payment link (UPI, NetBanking, Cards) will be sent straight to your registered email address.

1. Zero Advance Fee Model

All bookings placed through our mobile application or website are scheduled with zero advance deposit. The final quote is verified on-site by our lead technician based on actual physical square footage and material requirements before work starts.

2. Payment Terms & Digital Invoicing

Payment is due upon post-service inspection. Once you verify that every booked room and fixture meets our spotless criteria, our system generates and emails your GST tax invoice. You may settle the invoice instantly through the secure payment link provided in the email.

3. Rapid 90-Minute Express Dispatch

For on-demand rapid requests, our dispatch system assigns your closest technician squad for arrival within 90 minutes. In the rare event of traffic or extreme weather delays, our 24/7 Operations Desk will promptly notify you with real-time GPS updates.

4. 100% Satisfaction & 7-Day Rework Guarantee

If any corner, surface, or appliance cleaned under your work scope fails to satisfy our high standards, report it within 7 days of service completion. A quality supervisor will be dispatched for a complimentary re-clean at ₹0 extra fee.

5. Zero-Fee Cancellation & Rescheduling

You may reschedule or cancel your reservation anytime prior to specialist dispatch with absolutely zero cancellation fees. Cancellations requested when a specialist is already at your doorstep may carry a nominal conveyance charge of ₹149.

6. Non-Toxic, Child & Pet-Safe Formulations

We exclusively use biodegradable, non-corrosive, hospital-grade organic solutions. Our materials are completely safe for households with infants, elderly family members, and pets, without harsh chemical fumes or residues.

7. Privacy & Doorstep Security

Your phone number, address, and entry access instructions are secured by 256-bit encryption and are shared strictly with the assigned technician squad for service execution. Zero Spot never sells or shares customer information with external advertisers.
