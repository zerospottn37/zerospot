OUR STORY • [01]

Jewelry designed to become, over time, the rituals you hold on to.

Coutumes was founded by Edouard and Arthur. Passionate about well-made clothing, sharp cuts, and beautiful materials, they turned their attention to jewelry with one goal: to create the pieces they themselves wanted to wear.

A silver ring worn like a favorite shirt, a discreet necklace tucked under a tee-shirt, or a set stone piece that elevates an outfit. Simple, beautiful pieces, handmade in family-run workshops in Italy and France.

Crafted in 925 silver, brass, or natural stones, they bring subtle rhythm and character to everyday life. You wear them one day, set them aside, find them again the next.
